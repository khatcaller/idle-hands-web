Idle Hands privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

Idle Hands does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your device

Your best spinner speed, the secrets you've found, your chosen spinner skin and a few settings, such as sound on or off, are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage. They never leave your device, and deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes them.

Fonts

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website request, this sends your device's IP address to Google. See Google's privacy policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Children

Idle Hands is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/khatcaller/idle-hands-web. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
